# Ashcrown — Tone Bible

**Owner:** Creative Director  
**Status:** LOCKED for First Playable (§2)  
**Engine:** Godot 4 · mobile-first  
**Do not use:** Aetherwake, Ashen Crown branding

---

## 1. One sentence

Mythic wonder under a sky still glowing after fire — survivors rebuild with ash, light, and remnant machine-song. Not grimdark. Not goofy. Glory after the burn.

---

## 2. Ember / legacy (locked spine)

| Word | Means in Ashcrown |
|------|-------------------|
| **Ember** | Heat that remains after catastrophe — hope with weight. Warm lamps, ember undergrowth, Coilcrypt night glow, ash-gold UI accents. Never pure orange neon spam. |
| **Legacy** | What survived the Veil tear — Wake glyphs beside carved runes, heirloom weapons, NPCs who quote the player’s choices. History is quotable in one sentence, not a lore dump. |
| **Ashcrown** | Relic + era + player path as a **Waker**. Title must feel earned: ash (cost) + crown (claim). |

**Feeling test:** If a screen, VO line, or VFX could fit a generic dark fantasy or a cartoon idle game, rewrite it until it feels like dusk after a stellar fire — melancholy ruins beside bright living towns.

---

## 3. Emotional register

| Do | Don’t |
|----|-------|
| Quiet awe, dry humor, hard-won warmth | Edgy nihilism, meme banter, cartoon slapstick |
| Melancholy ruins *and* lively markets | All gray sludge or all candy saturation |
| Hope that costs something | Power fantasy with no scar |
| Tech as sacred remnant | Generic sci-fi HUD chrome |
| Magic as living will + ritual form | Sparkle spam with no coherence |

**Player fantasy:** “I woke with ash-light in my blood; the world remembers what I do — even when I’m not watching.”

---

## 4. Visual language (First Playable bar)

- **Palette:** Saturated dusk skies · bioluminescent Aether veins · ash-gold / ember accents · brushed Wake metal · deep forest greens · warm town lamps · cool marsh conduction glow.
- **Material contrast:** Living bark vs etched conduit; manuscript ornament vs clean HUD geometry.
- **Silhouettes first:** Landmarks and enemies must read at Mid preset on a phone before detail.
- **Light story:** Day = readable roam; dusk = brand hour; night = veins + Coilcrypt mouth + town lamps only (budget).
- **UI:** Mix manuscript flourishes with crisp combat HUD — never pure parchment clutter or pure sci-fi slabs.

**Forbidden for slice:** Pure cyberpunk neon cities, cartoon chibi mascots, blood-gore spectacle, illegible particle soup.

---

## 5. Voice & copy (player-facing)

- Short, concrete, slightly archaic verbs (“wake,” “bind,” “seal,” “charter”) — not thesaurus flex.
- Factions sound different: Concord = ledger/guard; Wakewrights = cold craft; Choirbound = hymn/warning.
- AFK report reads like a field log from a trusted agent — clear yields and choices, not joke spam.
- Boss and relic names carry weight: **Coil Warden**, **Ash-etched Circlet Fragment** — tease the crown, don’t hand it over in the tutorial.

---

## 6. Audio / VFX taste (directives)

- Ambience: wind through ash-gold canopy, soft conduit hum, town murmur — then combat punch.
- Combat tells: one dominant telegraph color per attack (see Combat Designer palette); no multi-hue fireworks.
- Ultimate / Wake skills: heat shimmer + ember trail, then cool-down silence — power has aftertaste.

---

## 7. Region lock (First Playable)

**Locked region:** **Emberveil Reach** (see `WORLD_FIRST_PLAYABLE.md`).  
Alternates (Corehold Marches, Sandglass Verge) shelved until Phase 5+.

Why Emberveil: one glance dual fantasy (living forest + buried Wake), contiguous mobile streaming, natural dusk brand hour, readable landmarks.

Before Creative Director signs First Playable:

2. Ember/legacy readable in art, UI, and copy without a design doc open.
3. Coil Warden fantasy matches Design Bible magic rules (binding wrong, not random demon).
4. No obsolete titles or Aetherwake leftovers in player-facing strings.
